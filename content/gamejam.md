The goal of the game jam is to design and implement games in AMOS Basic. AMOS Basic is a Basic dialect dedicated to the Amiga computer series. It allows making use of all the hardware capabilities of the Amiga computers. However, to simplify development and bring AMOS Basic to the ["masses, not the classes"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Tramiel), we developed CRVJA, an online editor and interpreter for [AMOS Basic](https://crvja.reanimate.school/), which we'll use to implement the games.

The game jam will be highly collaborative, with participants from diverse backgrounds paired to create games. From design to implementation, everyone'll be able to contribute and learn from others! Mentors from the humanities and engineering will drive the teams, while everyone'll have a chance to touch every aspect of the games.

For more info., don't hesitate to contact us!
