
# Revisiting PlayStation 1 Style and Aesthetic with TENUOUS:City and ShaderLab
## Frédérick Maheux

This presentation proposes a deep reading of two works by solo game developer Modus Interactive—TENUOUS:City (2021) and ShaderLab (2020)—that revisit the PlayStation 1 style and aesthetic. Both operate as liminal interactive objects, extending aesthetic potentials while exploring a nostalgic re-composition of alternative video game histories. They belong to Modus Interactive’s broader corpus, characterized by haunted digital houses, oneiric ambience, and glitches that manifest fragmented realities—a certain “ghost in the machine.” These two works are singular within that corpus in how explicitly they celebrate a specific era of video games: the PlayStation 1.
